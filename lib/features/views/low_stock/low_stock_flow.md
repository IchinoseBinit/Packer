step 3: Scan Carton QR code; if carton matches product then it will navigate to product scan screen

step 4: Scan required no. of product Qr code and the following product status will be updated to: Collected

step 5: Now go back to dashboard and click on top-right icon button to navigate to collected product screen

step 6: Click on details button on collected product screen

step 7: Scan basket QR code of that store to navigate to trolley item screen

step 8: Scan required no. of Product Qr code (Tags earlier scanned) and the following product will be submitted to server
